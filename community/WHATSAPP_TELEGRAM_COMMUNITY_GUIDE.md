# WhatsApp & Community Management Strategy 📱

**WhatsApp** is the primary platform for instant, high-reach student communications and community engagement at IUS.

---

## 1. Dual-Channel WhatsApp Architecture

### Channel A: The "Announcements Only" Channel (Broadcast / Topluluk Kanalı)
* **Settings**: Only Admins (Captain & PR Lead) can send messages.
* **Purpose**: Critical reminders that must never get lost in chat noise:
  * Room & time for tonight's workshop.
  * Hackathon application link deadlines.
  * Free pizza / catering alerts in campus amphitheaters.
* **Tone**: Short, bulleted, action-oriented with clear links.

### Channel B: The "General Discussion & Social" Group (Sohbet Grubu)
* **Settings**: Open to all active IUS members.
* **Purpose**: Daily networking, sharing campus news, memes, and finding study buddies.
* **Moderation Rules**:
  1. Respectful communication in English and Turkish/Bosnian.
  2. No spam, advertisements, or political discussions.
  3. Technical and project questions are coordinated during hands-on lab sessions and workshops.
