CONSTITUTION OF THE IUS ENGINEERING CLUB (IEC)

Pursuant to Article 77 of the Statute of the International University of Sarajevo (IUS) and the provisions of the Book of Rules on Establishing and Activities of Students' Clubs, the Founding Assembly of the IUS Engineering Club adopts the following official Constitution:

PART I: GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1: Name, Status, and Legal Affiliation

1. The official name of the student organization is "IUS Engineering Club" (hereinafter referred to as the "Club" or "IEC").

2. In Bosnian/Croatian/Serbian language, the name of the Club is "Inženjerski klub IUS".

3. The Club is an officially recognized, non-profit, student-led extracurricular organization established at the International University of Sarajevo (IUS).

4. The Club operates under the academic umbrella of the Faculty of Engineering and Natural Sciences (FENS) and is registered with and supervised by the Student & Career Center (SCC) (Building B, Ground Floor, Office G.18) in accordance with Student Club Registration Form (F252).

Article 2: Institutional Seat and Official Language

1. The official seat and headquarters of the Club is at the International University of Sarajevo:

Hrasnička Cesta 15, Ilidža 71210 Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina.

2. The primary working and documentation language of the Club is English. Official filings, university correspondence, and announcements may be conducted or translated into Bosnian/Croatian/Serbian as required by university administrative procedures.

Article 3: Visual Identity, Branding, and Public Communication

1. The Club shall have its own emblem, logo, and visual brand identity representing engineering, innovation, and university spirit.

2. In strict compliance with the IUS Rulebook on the Management of Information on University Websites and Social Media, all official social media channels, digital branding, flyers, and external announcements using the IUS name or logo must receive prior review and verification from the University Communications Office (UCO) and approval from the Faculty Academic Advisor.

3. No public promotion, campus poster campaigns, or official social media launches shall be initiated prior to receiving formal written registration approval from the Student & Career Center (SCC).

Article 4: Mission, Goals, and Scope of Activity

1. Mission: To create a dynamic engineering ecosystem that bridges theoretical academic education with real-world technical practice, competitive innovation, and industry integration.

2. Primary Operational Pillars & Objectives:

  • a. Weekly AI & Prompt Engineering Workshops: Organize regular, hands-on weekly practical labs focusing on artificial intelligence applications, effective prompt engineering, modern AI coding tools, and workflow automation.
  • b. Monthly On-Campus Tech Keynotes: Host prominent figures, engineering leaders, and technology founders for physical keynote sessions and Q&A panels in the IUS Amphitheater at least once every month.
  • c. Weekly Online Tech-Talks & Fireside Chats: Convene weekly interactive virtual discussions via Google Meet webinars with experienced software engineers, researchers, and domain experts globally to discuss real-world engineering practices and career paths.
  • d. Flagship Campus Hackathon (IUS DevHack): Organize an annual flagship 24-hour hackathon on campus, featuring company mentors, project problem sets, awards, and industry juries.
  • e. Annual On-Campus Technology Summit (IUS TechSummit): Organize a full-day university-wide engineering and technology conference on the IUS campus featuring prominent industry leaders, technology exhibition booths, keynote panels, and direct student career networking.
  • f. Volunteer Instructor & Peer-Mentorship Network: Establish an open volunteer instructor structure, recruiting capable senior students and external mentors to lead workshops and maintain continuous, sustainable peer-to-peer knowledge sharing.
  • g. Applied Engineering Project Showcases: Form and support multidisciplinary student teams developing practical engineering prototypes, software systems, and campus innovations.

    • Article 5: Non-Profit, Apolitical, and Non-Discriminatory Nature

    1. The Club is strictly non-profit. All financial assets, sponsorships, and material donations shall be used solely to realize club educational projects, workshops, competitions, and logistics.

    2. The Club is strictly apolitical and non-partisan. It shall not participate in, endorse, or host political party activities.

    3. The Club operates on principles of complete equality. Membership and leadership opportunities are open to all students regardless of race, nationality, gender, religion, sexual orientation, or physical disability.

    PART II: MEMBERSHIP

    Article 6: Membership Eligibility and Founding Quorum

    1. Any actively enrolled undergraduate, master's, or doctoral student at the International University of Sarajevo has the right to become a member of the Club.

    2. Pursuant to the IUS Book of Rules on Establishing and Activities of Students' Clubs, the official establishment of the Club requires an initial quorum of at least ten (10) currently enrolled IUS students registered on the official founding petition.

    Article 7: Membership Categories

    1. Active Members: Students who have completed the official registration form, participate in club activities, and exercise voting rights in the General Assembly.

    2. Committee Members: Active members who take on specific responsibilities within functional sub-committees (Software, Hardware, Logistics, PR, Competitions).

    3. Executive Board Members: The elected/appointed administrative officers of the Club.

    4. Alumni / Honorary Members: Graduated IUS students or technical mentors who provide advisory support without voting rights.

    Article 8: Rights and Duties of Members

    1. Rights:

  • To participate in all workshops, hackathons, seminars, and club field trips;
  • To access club technical hardware, project equipment, and workspace allocations;
  • To vote and run for positions in the General Assembly and executive bodies in accordance with constitutional criteria;
  • To propose new engineering projects, study groups, or workshop topics.

    • 2. Duties:

  • To respect the Statute of IUS, the IUS Code of Ethics, and the IUS Student Code of Conduct;
  • To safeguard university and club property, laboratory instruments, and facilities;
  • To represent the Club and the International University of Sarajevo with academic and ethical integrity.

    • Article 9: Termination of Membership

    1. Membership shall terminate upon:

  • Voluntary written resignation submitted to the General Secretary;
  • Graduation or termination of student status at IUS;
  • Disciplinary expulsion approved by a two-thirds (2/3) majority of the Executive Board for severe violation of university regulations, ethical misconduct, or unauthorized actions damaging the reputation of the University or the Club.

    • PART III: BODIES OF THE CLUB AND GOVERNANCE

    Article 10: Governing Bodies

    The administrative and governing bodies of the Club are:

    1. The General Assembly;

    2. The Executive Management Board;

    3. The Functional Working Committees;

    4. The Faculty Academic Advisor.

    Article 11: The General Assembly

    1. The General Assembly is the supreme deliberative body of the Club and consists of all registered active members.

    2. The Regular General Assembly convenes at least once per academic semester. An Extraordinary General Assembly may be convened by decision of the Executive Board or upon written request of at least one-third (1/3) of active members.

    3. The General Assembly:

  • Reviews and evaluates the annual activity report and financial statement;
  • Discusses strategic initiatives and long-term directions;
  • Confirms constitutional amendments and dissolution proposals.

    • 4. Decisions are adopted by a simple majority vote of members present, provided a quorum of at least one-third of registered active members is present.

    Article 12: The Executive Management Board

    1. The Executive Management Board is the executive governing body responsible for the day-to-day administration, planning, project execution, and official representation of the Club.

    2. The Executive Board consists of four (4) core officers:

  • President
  • Vice President
  • General Secretary
  • Treasurer

    • 3. The term of office for Executive Board members is one (1) academic year, with the possibility of re-election.

    Article 13: Duties and Criteria of Executive Officers

    1. President (Predsjednik Kluba):

  • Mandatory Institutional Criterion: In accordance with the IUS Student & Career Center (SCC) regulations, the President of a student club must be an enrolled IUS student in at least the second (2nd) year of study.
  • Officially represents the Club before the IUS Rectorate, FENS Dean's Office, Student & Career Center (SCC), Student Parliament (SPIUS), and external partners.
  • Signs official university petitions, event reservation requests, and formal correspondence together with the Faculty Academic Advisor.
  • Convenes and chairs regular Executive Board meetings.
  • Appointed Officer: Kaan Mete Şenyıldız (Student ID: 250302201, 2nd Year FENS).

    • 2. Vice President (Potpredsjednik Kluba):

  • Assists the President in the general administration and operational coordination of the Club.
  • Coordinates the workflows of the functional committees and monitors project milestones.
  • Exercises the duties and representation authority of the President in his temporary absence.
  • Appointed Officer: Hasan Kaan (Student ID: 250302195, FENS).

    • 3. General Secretary (Generalni Sekretar):

  • Prepares and maintains official minutes of Executive Board and General Assembly meetings.
  • Maintains the official Register of Members and founding records.
  • Manages official administrative correspondence and coordinates document submissions with SCC Office G.18.
  • Appointed Officer: Mahmut İhsan Avcı (Student ID: 250302233, FENS).

    • 4. Treasurer (Blagajnik):

  • Prepares the annual Draft Budget Estimate in coordination with the Executive Board.
  • Manages the financial ledger, records event expenditures, and collects all receipts, invoices, and sponsorship documentation.
  • Compiles the semi-annual and annual financial transparency reports for the Executive Board and the SCC.
  • Appointed Officer: Bekir Enes Çokbekler (Student ID: 250302229, FENS).

    • Article 14: Functional Working Committees (Odbori)

    To ensure structured execution, the Executive Board establishes functional committees:

    1. Committee on Software Engineering & Artificial Intelligence: Oversees software workshops, coding labs, web/app development, and hackathon technical problem design.

    2. Committee on Hardware, Robotics & Multidisciplinary Labs: Coordinates embedded systems, microcontrollers (Arduino/STM32/ESP32), 3D printing, and IoT prototyping.

    3. Committee on Applied Projects & Prototypes: Coordinates student project teams, hardware/software prototypes, and semester showcases.

    4. Committee on Public Relations, Media & Branding: Coordinates visual graphic design, photography, video coverage, and compliance with UCO guidelines.

    5. Committee on Logistics & Operations: Manages amphitheatre bookings, audio-visual technical setups, catering coordination, and event hospitality.

    PART IV: FACULTY ACADEMIC ADVISOR

    Article 15: Appointment and Role of the Advisor

    1. Pursuant to the IUS Book of Rules on Establishing and Activities of Students' Clubs, the Club must have an appointed Faculty Academic Advisor chosen from the full-time academic staff of the Faculty of Engineering and Natural Sciences (FENS).

    2. The Academic Advisor:

  • Provides academic, ethical, and pedagogical counsel to the student leadership;
  • Reviews and endorses the Annual Activity Plan and Draft Budget Estimate prior to submission to the SCC;
  • Endorses official facility reservation requests and overnight laboratory or campus access;
  • Assists in resolving organizational questions and serves as an institutional liaison with the FENS Dean's Office and University Rectorate.

    • PART V: PLANNING, CAMPUS LOGISTICS, AND SAFETY

    Article 16: Annual Activity Plan and SCC Registration Deadline

    1. In accordance with university directives, the Executive Board must formulate and submit a comprehensive Annual Activity Plan to the Student & Career Center (SCC) before the university deadline (late November).

    2. The Activity Plan shall encompass all scheduled workshops, bootcamps, guest lectures, hackathons, and community meetings for the academic year.

    Article 17: Campus Facilities, Room Bookings, and Safety Protocols

    1. Reservations for campus premises (e.g., Main Amphitheatre, FENS Computer Labs, Research Centers, Campus Courtyard) must be filed through the official SCC event reservation procedures at least ten (10) working days prior to the event.

    2. For specialized events requiring extended or overnight campus hours (such as the 24-hour IUS DevHack), explicit written authorizations from the FENS Dean's Office, Campus Security, and the IT Center must be secured in advance.

    3. Members and participants must treat all university laboratory equipment, computing assets, and premises with care and responsibility, adhering to campus safety rules.

    PART VI: FINANCIAL ASSETS AND TRANSPARENCY

    Article 18: Sources of Funds and Financial Management

    1. The Club operates under a diversified, sustainable multi-tier financial framework. Financial resources may be derived from:

  • Corporate Sponsorships & Industry Partnerships: Financial and material support from technology enterprises, IT scaleups, and ecosystem partners;
  • Institutional University Allocations & Grants: Discretionary student club activity funding, event co-sponsorship, and logistical grants provided by the IUS Student & Career Center (SCC) and the Faculty of Engineering and Natural Sciences (FENS);
  • Contingency Participant Micro-Contributions: Modest, nominal participation fees (e.g., approximately 5 BAM) levied selectively for resource-intensive, high-cost flagship events (such as 24-hour campus hackathons requiring multi-meal catering and hardware supplies), strictly in instances where corporate sponsorships and university grants leave an operational deficit;
  • In-Kind & Material Donations: Educational dev boards, software licenses, cloud computing credits, and event catering.

    • 2. Regular weekly workshops, technical training labs, guest keynotes, and general assembly meetings shall remain strictly free of charge for all IUS students.

    3. All revenues, donations, and expenditures must be transparently documented in the club ledger with corresponding receipts by the Club Treasurer.

    4. The Club is strictly non-profit. No member, officer, or advisor shall derive personal financial gain or profit from club activities. All assets and funds shall be exclusively deployed for student educational and organizational enrichment.

    PART VII: AMENDMENTS AND DISSOLUTION

    Article 19: Constitutional Amendments

    1. Amendments to this Constitution may be proposed by the Executive Board or by a written petition signed by at least one-third of active members.

    2. Amendments require approval by a two-thirds (2/3) majority of the Executive Board, consent from the Faculty Academic Advisor, and formal notification to the Student & Career Center (SCC).

    Article 20: Dissolution and Liquidation of Assets

    1. The Club may cease its activities:

  • Upon a resolution approved by a three-fourths (3/4) majority of the General Assembly;
  • By administrative decision of the IUS Rectorate or SCC in the event of persistent inactivity, failure to re-register for the academic year, or grave violation of university regulations.

    • 2. In the event of dissolution, all physical equipment, robotics kits, computing components, and remaining financial assets of the Club shall be transferred to the Faculty of Engineering and Natural Sciences (FENS) / Student & Career Center (SCC) for the educational benefit of future engineering students.

    PART VIII: FINAL AND RATIFICATION PROVISIONS

    Article 21: Entry into Force

    This Constitution enters into force on the day of its formal adoption by the Founding Assembly and subsequent registration with the IUS Student & Career Center (SCC).
