IUS PowerHub: Smart Campus Station Flow

A completely student-engineered, RFID-authenticated powerbank network across IUS classrooms, the central library, and cafeterias. Zero commercial cost, self-charging, and circular campus technology.

💳
STEP 01

Tap IUS Card

Hold your student ID card against the RFID/NFC sensor on the station. Instant cryptographic student UID verification.

🔓
STEP 02

Instant Unlock

Green LED status light illuminates. The electromagnetic solenoid lock clicks open, smoothly releasing a 100% charged powerbank.

⚡
STEP 03

Charge Anywhere

Take the slim battery pack to your lecture amphi, computer lab, or library desk. Fast-charging with integrated USB-C & Lightning cables.

🔄
STEP 04

Return & Auto-Charge

Snap the powerbank back into any open dock slot. Magnetic pogo-pin rails lock the unit and trigger automated high-efficiency recharge.
