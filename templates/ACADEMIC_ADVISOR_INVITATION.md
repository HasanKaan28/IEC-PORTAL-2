On behalf of the founding student initiative of the **IUS Engineering Club**, we are writing to respectfully invite you to serve as our **Academic Faculty Advisor** for the upcoming academic year.

### About the Club
The IUS Engineering Club has been established by a dedicated group of engineering students with the mission to bridge academic theory and practical engineering excellence. Our core activities include:
1. Organizing hands-on technical workshops in modern Software Development, Artificial Intelligence, and Embedded Hardware.
2. Mentoring student engineering project sprints, coding bootcamps, and practical project showcases.
3. Hosting university-wide hackathons and tech talks with industry leaders from Sarajevo and abroad.
4. Fostering multidisciplinary collaboration across all FENS programs.

### Role of the Academic Advisor
As our Faculty Advisor, your involvement would be invaluable in guiding our academic direction, ensuring alignment with university regulations, and providing high-level mentorship. Specifically, the role entails:
* Acting as our academic sponsor and institutional liaison with the FENS Dean’s Office and Student Affairs (SKS).
* Advising on the technical quality and educational value of our planned workshops and hackathons.
* Attending key milestone gatherings, such as our Semester Welcome Meeting and Flagship Hackathon jury panels, as your schedule permits.

We believe that under your esteemed guidance, this student club will significantly elevate student engagement, technical skills, and the academic reputation of IUS.

We would be deeply honored to discuss this initiative with you in person at your earliest convenience. Thank you very much for your time, support, and dedication to student development.
